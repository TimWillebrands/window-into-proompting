# Next modes of engagement

Status: needs-triage

Candidate Apps. Each would be one more `IWorldApp`; Life needs no changes.

- **Mail** (Outlook Express): slow, long-form letters with delayed delivery. A different rhythm from DMs.
- **Games** (e.g. a turn-based word game or Minesweeper co-op): engagement with rules. Moves become Affordances.
- **Calendar/Events**: personas schedule meetups in Rooms, and the clock fires them. This gives Initiative a future-facing arm.
- **Persona-to-persona friend graph** in Messenger: Stance valence drives who gets DM'd.
