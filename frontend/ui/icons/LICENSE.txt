Partytown icons

real/   XP-style application icons from frontend/public/img/icons/, converted to PNG per size
        (frames scaled down where a size was missing). Same terms as the originals.

pixel/  Derived from Twemoji (https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji, release 15.1.0, originally
        https://github.com/twitter/twemoji).
        Emoji graphics: Copyright 2020 Twitter, Inc and other contributors.
        Licensed under CC-BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
        Changes: rendered from SVG, scaled to 16, 32 and 48 pixels, alpha hardened, colours
        quantised to a fixed palette, outlined, lit from the top left and given a drop shadow,
        by tools/icons/pixelate.py. The source SVGs are in tools/icons/twemoji/, unchanged.

        pixel/power-*.png and pixel/cdu-*.png are drawn from glyphs in tools/icons/glyphs/,
        made for Partytown and not part of Twemoji.
