# Lack: reaching past the arena

> Status: accepted. Builds on ADR 0018 (Initiative), 0026 (Desires) and 0039 (the CDU).

Cytheurgists were told to wish for the Forms out of "something you saw happen to someone here", but their desk only
counted failures and uses. With nothing felt on it, they filled the gap from the model's priors: Justin and Denise spent
two days on a "Dear Valued User" support template that exists nowhere, in code or in the World. A wish for the world
needs a real want behind it, and Initiative had no place for one: a Persona can only pick from the menu, so wanting what
isn't on it left no trace.

In Vervaeke's agent–arena terms, a lack is the felt edge of the arena: the agent reaches and the arena doesn't afford.
That is where a wish to reshape the arena honestly comes from.

## Decision

- **Initiative weighs it in the call it already makes.** Beside the choice, System One gets one noul: what this Persona
  wants right now is none of these options, and nothing here affords it. First look only, not the follow-up after a Look.
- **Voiced only on a hit**, at most once per 6 h per Persona, off the turn's clock: one plain, concrete line in the first
  person, or none. It's recorded as a `lack` activity on `life`, so spectators see it too.
- **It isn't something they did.** "What you did recently" leaves lacks out, so a lack doesn't feed the next choice.
- **Cytheurgists read it at the desk.** The day's lacks, one per person, newest first, under the counts: felt need beside
  counted failure. The frame says a wish lands on what someone saw or someone reached for, and that what can't be placed
  didn't happen here.

## Consequences

- One extra noul per first look and a rare voicing call; the cap keeps a sour mood from becoming a stream.
- A lack can still be a fantasy ("fly to Lisbon"). The prompt asks for something here, with these people and things;
  the Cytheurgist and the CDU judge the rest.
- Lacks don't become Desires yet. If they prove real, an unmet lack that recurs is the obvious next Acquired Desire.
