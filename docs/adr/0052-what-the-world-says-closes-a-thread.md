# What the World says closes a thread

> Status: accepted. Amends ADR 0021 (decision 20, life threads). Builds on 0019/0044 (System One), 0026 (desire review),
> 0039 and 0051 (the CDU).

A Day is written by one sealed call. It gets the date, places, yesterday's mood and Preoccupation, the carried threads,
wants and fixed beats, and nothing of what happened. A thread ended only when that same call wrote `done: …`, or after
8 days. Nothing the World said could reach it, and the Preoccupation carried the same content into the next Day.

For staff of the CDU that collided with reality (10–12 Nov). Denise's Preoccupation was the staging numbers for a
release schedule review, Justin's a VAT form; neither exists in the World or the repo. 24 of 30 desk exchanges were
premised on such things, and the CDU said a flat no in 21. Denise said "drop it" at 17:08 and asked for the staging
numbers again at 17:19. The no was captured as her work memory; it never reached her Day. Ask before brief (0051)
works; the source was upstream of the desk.

## Decision

1. **Rest reads threads against what happened** (`ThreadReview`). On any rest or diary turn, at most every 3 h, System
   One reads the Participant's recalled Recollections (up to 8, anchored on the threads) against each thread and the
   Preoccupation, two nouls each: *done or settled now*, and *isn't so* (doesn't exist, can't happen here, isn't theirs).
   At 0.7 either one closes it. Same shape as the desire review (0026): rest decides what things came to.
2. **Closed is gone.** A closed thread leaves the threads, and its age with it. A closed Preoccupation is cleared, so
   yesterday's line no longer carries it and it can't reseed the thread. Settled ones read as "Finally settled" until
   the next Day. Contradicted ones are not written anywhere: naming the fiction again would prime it.
3. **Nothing remembered, nothing judged.** Without Recollections there is nothing to read against, so no call; a
   missing answer is no view and keeps the thread.
4. **Staff Days get the desk as state.** When the Occupation names a Company of kind `cdu`, the Day call is handed
   where the work stands, fetched fresh: the commits on the trunk, the Agora PR and its checks, and the desk's last
   three answers for that Company. It says this is all that is underway there. The prompt gains one line: work beats
   and work threads come from "At work". No list of what not to write.

## Considered

- **A ban list in the Day prompt** ("no staging, no VAT"). Names the motif on every Day and pushes the next fiction
  somewhere else.
- **Recollections in the Day call.** Every Day would carry recall's picks, and a sealed call that writes the life
  shouldn't also judge it. The review is the one place that asks.
- **Reviewing just before the Day.** Puts a judge call on the first prompt of the morning; rest comes often enough.
- **Trading desks.** Their Book is facts too, but nobody invented trades; `cdu` first.

## Consequences

- One System One call per Participant per rest, at most every 3 h, and only while they have threads.
- A Cytheurgist's beats now name the trunk, the Agora and the CDU in their own Right now; keeping the secret stays
  theirs (0039).
- A dark CDU leaves only the desk's answers in the facts.
- The 0.7 bar is the desire review's; if real Days show threads closing on a hunch, raise it.
