# The desk is a window and a brief

> Status: accepted. Amends ADR 0039, decisions 5, 6 and 10. Builds on 0047 (Lack).

Under 0039 a Cytheurgist "wished" and the CDU decided what to build. Staff arrived at the desk with a want already formed and no way to find out what exists, so they invented: a support greeting, staging boxes, a vesting schema. The CDU audited the absence, and the audit went to the Agora as PR #125. A supplicant is a role with no craft in the weights; an engineer directing a coding agent is one with a great deal.

## Decision

1. **The frame is engineering, the lore a thin coat.** A Cytheurgist is an engineer who can't open a file or run a query. The CDU is their only window on how the world is built and their only hands in it. Forms, tide, Agora and the One stay as house names for code, push, PR and reviewer.
2. **Two ways to write to it.** `ask`: one question about what is built, stored or happened; the CDU answers from code and data and changes nothing. `talk`: a brief for one change, saying what should differ, where it showed up, and how both will know it's done. `answer` is unchanged.
3. **The CDU says what is built, not what to build.** Its answers become staff's work memory, which is how they come to know their world. What to change is theirs to decide, among themselves.
4. **Their own answer gets room.** A Cytheurgist reads up to 1,500 characters of the CDU's reply to them and 500 of a colleague's; the soul holds an answer to ten plain lines.
5. **Looking before "not found".** The soul carries how to read grain state by text, and asks for what was searched and how many rows came back. It had told a Cytheurgist that a living Persona wasn't in the data.
6. **A spent allowance parks, and says whose it was.** Writing to a spent CDU isn't hidden: the message stays on their desk unsent, and they remember it with the day's spend per person. The desk shows that split all day. Once parked, the verbs go until the allowance returns; anything new they write replaces it.
7. **Sharing is stated as how it is here.** The desk names who the CDU is shared with and that it's one allowance, one trunk and one PR. No rule makes them talk first; the shared budget and trunk are the reason to.

## Considered

- **A tool loop at the desk** (read tools, one write). The right shape for agents, but the CDU's turns take minutes and a Life turn comes every twenty: delegating and coming back is already the natural rhythm. Parked.
- **To-dos and a mission document per Company.** A written list re-injects a confabulated motif every turn with its author's authority; the Mandate already is the mission.
- **Deliberation primitives** (matters, positions, decisions). That is cognition's work, not state.
- **Sending a parked message when the allowance returns.** It would spend the morning's first money on yesterday's thought. They see it and choose.

## Consequences

- An old CDU reads `ask` as `talk`: harmless, but it may build on a question until it's redeployed.
- Spend per person is the Meter's number per answered exchange; tide and watch costs belong to nobody and aren't shown.
- `CONTEXT.md` still describes a Cytheurgist's moves as talk, answer, look.
