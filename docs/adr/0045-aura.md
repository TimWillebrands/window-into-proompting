# Aura

> Status: accepted. Amends ADR 0021 (#5) and ADR 0031.

Other people's prompts only knew someone by name, where they stand and their own history with them. That is on
purpose: bios in the roster leaked one persona's theme and register into the others (`SpeakingSession`). But it means
nobody can come across as anything before they've done something. A Player who plays an outsider can't make the room
feel it.

At the same time, every human was labelled: "(human)" in chat rosters, "(a real person)" in every Reflex event. That
marks Players as apart from the world in a way they didn't choose, while what they did choose had no way in.

## Decision

- **An Aura is one line on the Persona**, up to 64 characters, empty by default: how they come across up close.
  Players set their own (`PUT worlds/{id}/player/aura`, Wardrobe), admins any Persona's (persona editor).
- **It's felt only at the same Spot.** Places mirrors it onto the Body. The situation line for whoever is right next
  to you gets "up close: …", and walking up to someone adds "Up close: …" to their Reflex event. Nothing across the room,
  in chats or in the roster: closeness is what makes it a perception rather than a bio.
- **Nobody is labelled human.** The roster shows names (the Narrator stays marked), and Reflex events drop
  "(a real person)". The human norms stay in Reflex's maths (ADR 0031: never left on read, owed within 12 minutes);
  they just aren't text the persona reads.

## Consequences

- An Aura is player-written text in other personas' prompts. Its length and place keep it a nudge, not an
  instruction; long-form identity stays out of other people's prompts, as before.
- Personas can no longer tell from the prompt who is a person. Behaviour toward humans now rests on the mechanics
  alone.
